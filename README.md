A program that sums up the world news of Wednesday, 25 November 2026 in one newspaper-style image: AI shares tumbling a day after Nvidia's 31.5% surge, Ukrainian drone strikes on Russian ships in the Gulf of Finland, the first Pakistani in orbit aboard Shenzhou-24, and Huawei found guilty on 10 of 12 counts. It validates the poster PNG and writes it to /output/entry.png.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The poster itself is drawn by `draw_poster.py` (PIL) and copied from the repository by `main.py`.
